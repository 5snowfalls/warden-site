What it does

Three jobs, one bot.

No bloated menu of features you'll never touch — just the parts of running a server that actually take up your time.

Moderation

Kick, ban, timeout, and warn — logged, reversible where it matters, and gone the moment you decide it's over.

/kick/ban/unban/timeout/warn/clear/roleadd/roleremove/role/mute/unmute/softban/slowmode/lock/unlock/purgeuser

Leveling

Members earn XP for showing up and talking, not for spamming. A leaderboard gives your regulars something to watch.

/rank/leaderboard

Utility

The small lookups people ask a mod for at 2am — server stats, join dates, latency — answered instantly instead.

/serverinfo/userinfo/ping/help

Security

Anti-invite links, anti-mass-mention, and auto-timeout spam protection — configurable per server.

/automod

Fun & Roleplay

Anime-style action gifs, roasts, compliments, and quick games — the commands people actually spam in chat.

Economy

A simple coin economy — earn daily and from work, pay other members, and climb the richest-members board.

/balance/daily/work/pay/leaderboard-coins

Server Tools

Welcome and leave messages, autorole for new members, and reaction-based giveaways.

/setwelcome/setleave/setautorole/giveaway

Prefix

Change Warden's text-command prefix per server, for anyone who still prefers typing over slash commands.

/setprefix

Live Updates

Pull the latest code and reload every command instantly — no restart, no downtime.

/update/rebuild/restart
In practice

Every action, accounted for.

Warden replies the same way every time: who did what, to whom, and why — so nobody has to ask "wait, who warned them?"

  • → Warnings stack per member, with a full history on request.
  • → Every moderation action names the moderator, never just "the bot."
  • → Level-ups announce themselves — no need to check a leaderboard to notice.
